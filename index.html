What is Influenza?

Influenza, commonly known as the flu, is a viral infection that affects the respiratory system. It can cause mild to severe illness, and sometimes can lead to hospitalization.

Symptoms

  • Fever
  • Cough
  • Runny or stuffy nose
  • Body aches
  • Headache
  • Fatigue

How to Prevent Influenza

Wash your hands regularly, avoid close contact with sick people, and get vaccinated every year.

What to Do if You're Sick

Stay at home, rest, drink plenty of fluids, and consult a doctor if symptoms worsen.
