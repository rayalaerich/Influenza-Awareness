What is Influenza?

Influenza, commonly known as the flu, is a contagious respiratory illness caused by influenza viruses. It can cause mild to severe symptoms, including fever, cough, sore throat, muscle aches, and fatigue. Getting vaccinated each year and practicing good hygiene are the best ways to protect yourself and others from the flu.

Common Symptoms of Influenza

Fever

Cough

Sore Throat

Headache

Muscle Pain

Fatigue

How to Prevent Influenza

Wash your hands frequently.

Wear a face mask in crowded places.

Get your flu vaccine every year.

Cover your mouth when coughing or sneezing.

Stay home and rest when you’re sick.

What To Do If You’re Sick?

If you start showing influenza symptoms, follow these simple steps to recover and avoid spreading the virus.

Stay at Home

Rest and avoid close contact with others to prevent spreading infection.

Take Medicine

Use fever reducers or cough medicine as advised by your doctor.

Call a Doctor

Seek medical help if symptoms get worse or if you have breathing difficulties.
